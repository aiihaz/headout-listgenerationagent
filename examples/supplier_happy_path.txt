SUPPLIER: Desert Adventures LLC
EXPERIENCE: Dubai Desert Safari with Dune Bashing, Camel Ride & BBQ Dinner

OVERVIEW
Our flagship evening desert safari departs daily from Dubai city hotels at 3:30 PM.
Guests are collected from their hotel lobby and driven 45 minutes to our private desert
camp on the outskirts of Al Marmoom Desert Conservation Reserve.

The safari includes approximately 45 minutes of dune bashing in 4x4 Land Cruisers driven
by our professional guides. Following dune bashing, guests can ride our friendly camels
and try sandboarding on the dunes. At sunset (typically around 6:15 PM), the camp opens
for a traditional Bedouin-style BBQ buffet dinner featuring grilled meats, mezze, rice,
salads and non-alcoholic beverages. Entertainment includes belly dancing and a Tanoura
show. Shisha (flavoured tobacco) is available at additional cost.

Total duration: approximately 6 hours. Return drop-off to hotels by 9:30 PM.

IMPORTANT NOTES
- Dune bashing is not recommended for pregnant women, people with back problems, heart
  conditions, or those who have recently had surgery. Our guides will assess on the day.
- Children under 3 must be secured in a child seat (we provide these on request, please
  inform us in advance).
- The camp has basic toilet facilities. A small prayer room is available.
- Guests should wear comfortable, modest clothing. Light layers recommended for the evening.
- Photography and videography permitted throughout. Our photographer is available for
  professional shots at AED 200 (USB drive included).
